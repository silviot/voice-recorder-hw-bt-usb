## High severity

1. **The packetizer assumes an SBC configuration the advertised endpoints do not guarantee.** Sections 3.5–4.4 hard-code 128 PCM frames per SBC frame and the joint-stereo byte count. A peer can select four blocks and four subbands from the advertised caps, yielding 16 PCM frames. Dual channel also changes packet length. This affects allocation size, RTP timestamps, pacing and rewind together. Derive all four from the accepted configuration. Test peer-initiated configurations and an MTU too small for one frame. The vendored source-cap validator requires advertising all four block lengths. Evidence: `btc_av.c:2604`, `bta_av_co_audio_media_supports_config`, `SBC_Encoder`, `EncPacking`.

2. **The external queue can report success after losing a packet, and the proposed retry can stop playback permanently.** `btc_a2dp_source_enqueue_audio_frame` ignores the Boolean result of `fixed_queue_enqueue`; a list-node allocation failure leaks the buffer and produces a false `ESP_OK`. Separately, §4.3 waits for another STARTED after ordinary queue-full `ESP_FAIL`. A draining queue on an already-started stream produces no such event. Propagate enqueue failure, retain the encoded packet for a later tick, and handle allocation failure before consuming PCM. Re-encoding saved PCM also advances SBC analysis history twice. Evidence: `btc_a2dp_source_ext_codec.c:78`, `common/osi/fixed_queue.c:143`, `btc_av.c:3716`, `sbc_encoder.c:SBC_Encoder`.

3. **Controller completions cannot supply the promised file position.** `bt_ctrl.c:rom_nocp` receives an ACL handle and count, with no media CID, packet identity or playback status. A signalling completion or a late completion after resume can therefore credit new media despite the saturation rule. The A2DP API handle is a BTA handle, so matching it directly to the ACL handle would also fail. Even perfect media-completion counts include silence and have no seek, resume or file origin. Keep real source-frame ranges and a playback generation per packet. Use controller progress for the watchdog and describe audible position as an estimate. Cover seek, seek-back, inter-file silence and late completions in S10. Evidence: `bt_ctrl.c:474`, `btc_av.c:btc_a2d_src_audio_data_send`, `player.c:player_heard_frames`, `player_seek_to_frame`.

4. **Just Works bypasses the pairing window and key-reuse checks.** `btc_dm_sp_cfm_req_evt` calls `BTA_DmConfirm(..., true)` before the app sees anything. `btc_dm_auth_cmpl_evt` stores the key before notifying GAP. An incoming-address filter cannot cover outgoing pairing, a previously bonded headset that lost its key, or a window that expires after admission. Post-hoc removal also misses the phone path’s accepted-then-failed key burn. Make the pre-confirm decision mandatory before H3 pairing. Separately, reset or select the in-memory bond store on every mode change: `bt_port_init` is one-shot and `bonds_load` leaves the previous RAM cache intact on missing SD or file. Two filenames alone do not isolate modes. Evidence: `btc_dm.c:417,581`, `bt_xrec_bluedroid.c:on_cfm_req`, `ssp_burn`, `bt_port_glue.c:bt_port_init`, `bt_xrec_fw.c:bonds_load`.

5. **Alarm cancellation and the 500 ms timeout do not establish safe teardown.** Alarm generation changes on set/cancel/free. It does not acknowledge callback completion. `alarm_cb_handler` releases its mutex before invoking the callback. A2DP deinit itself issues close/disconnect, and `clean_up` retains responsibility for the queue and state machines until `BTA_AV_DISABLE_EVT`. Continuing after 500 ms leaves that ownership unresolved. Stop and acknowledge the producer on BTC, keep the deadline outside the blocking worker, and cover the complete controller-delivery drain and host deinit on the frozen-peer path. Evidence: `bt_alarm.c:bt_alarm_drain`, `common/osi/alarm.c:alarm_cb_handler`, `btc_av.c:btc_a2d_request_teardown`, `clean_up`, `bt_xrec_fw.c:job_down`.

6. **The ring flush grants space before the consumer relinquishes it.** In §3.3 a producer can flush a full ring and overwrite a slot while the consumer is copying it. `volatile` plus `__DMB()` does not establish C synchronization for the proposed pthread/TSan test, and ordinary `max(rd, flush_wr)` fails across wrap. Publish indices with acquire/release semantics and reclaim discarded slots only after consumer acknowledgement. Include a consumer paused mid-copy in H1. Also pass the file channel count at `aout_start`; a stereo scrub write can be the first write for a mono file. Evidence: design §§3.1–3.3, `player.c:522,644`, `audio_dac.c:g_dac_buf`.

7. **The requested AVRCP Target notifications are unsupported by this vendor tree.** `cs_rn_allowed_evt` is `0x2000` (volume only). Requesting PLAY_STATUS_CHANGE and TRACK_CHANGE returns `ESP_ERR_NOT_SUPPORTED`, and the response builder implements only volume. Passthrough transport keys can still work. Either narrow v1 to those controls or budget a vendor change and test registration, INTERIM and CHANGED on the wire. Evidence: `btc_avrc.c:263,2161`, `esp_avrc_api.c:561`.

## Medium severity

8. **The clock is usable, but the pacing equation and BTC workload are wrong as written.** `esp_timer_get_time` reaches the 64-bit hardware microsecond counter. Converting elapsed microseconds to PCM frames requires division by 1,000,000 and a carried remainder; the stated equation reaches the clamp on the first tick. The internal encoder’s alarm only posts work to a separate source task. Encoding up to two packets inside BTC changes stack demand and delays other alarms and AVRCP callbacks. Gate worst-case BTC callback latency and stack headroom under simultaneous control traffic, alongside average CPU. Evidence: `bt_port_glue.c:plat_now_us`, `wdt_timer.c:us_timer_now`, `btc_a2dp_source.c:btc_a2dp_source_alarm_cb`, `btc_task.c:336`.

9. **Asynchronous media control needs failure transitions.** A failed or busy START leaves the player filling a ring nobody drains. A rejected SUSPEND leaves the stream running after the design has stopped its tick. Neither requires a disconnect, and a watchdog conditioned on `down_sbc > 0` can miss both. Give START/SUSPEND a bounded pending state, handle failed ACKs explicitly, and test REC during a rejected suspend. Evidence: `btc_a2dp_control.c:108–171`, `btc_a2dp_source_ext_codec.c:btc_a2dp_source_on_suspended`, `player.c:885`.

10. **The peak RAM estimate omits the largest new PCM buffer.** The §4.3 holdover at 11 frames per packet is `11 × 128 × 2 × 2 = 5,632` bytes. The general FIR’s 1,024 Q15 coefficients alone are 2,048 bytes against a 1.3 KB allowance. Packet heap also includes queue nodes and data-ready messages. Retaining one encoded packet removes most of the holdover cost. Put constant taps in flash and measure largest free block during streaming congestion in H3. Evidence: design §§3.4, 4.3, 12.1, `fixed_queue.c:fixed_queue_enqueue`, `bta_av_ci.c:bta_av_ci_src_data_ready`, `sl6801_firm.ld:.bt_bss`.

11. **H3 can pass without establishing the claimed radio throughput.** Exp 4c uses a 16 kHz tone, which selects speech bitpool 24 while the experiment sweeps advertised maxima 35/45/53. Disable the speech override for that sweep and verify the encoded header at the receiver. `open.edr` comes from remote feature bits and cannot prove on-air EDR. Also, the proposed `hp_credits` setting must change both the synthetic `1005` reply and the shim’s submission limit. Gate sustained receiver-measured throughput and gaps against the actual encoded rate. Evidence: design §§3.5, 10.2, 11, `sbc_packing.c:91`, `bta_av_aact.c:1483`, `bt_ctrl_cmds.c:341`, `bt_ctrl.c:1177`.

12. **Several milestone gates contradict their prerequisites or the single-stream code.** H3 requires S2 to name exactly two audio devices, but the CoD name-skip patch lands in H5; move it forward or change H3’s expectation. S7 requires a BTA list drop, but `bta_av_data_path` removes the existing head before checking whether three entries remain, and only reads fresh source data when the list is empty. Single-stream congestion alone does not establish that drop path. S8 promises new audio within two packets even though §4.4 allows twelve downstream. Gate against observed queue occupancy and distinguish induced drops from ordinary congestion. Finally, rebase patch numbering: the current patch README already allocates 0029–0033. Evidence: `btc_gap_bt.c:325`, `bta_av_aact.c:bta_av_data_path`, design §§4.5, 8.2, 13, `open_fw/third_party/bluedroid_patches/README.md`.
