## Functional Description

*(Manual note: "To purchase different models, press the buttons in the figure above to define the operation.")*

### Charging

1. **Charge through the adapter:** Connect the adapter to the power supply before charging, then plug one end of the USB cable into the adapter and connect the USB interface of the recorder at the other end. It can be fully charged in about **2 hours** under normal conditions. Use a **5V 1000 mA** power adapter that meets national standards.
2. **Charging via computer USB:** Insert one end of the standard USB cable into the computer USB socket and the other end into the recorder USB socket to charge. At the same time, you can use the recorder as a USB mass-storage disk ("mobile disk"). Charging via computer USB takes longer than using a wall adapter.
3. **Notes:**
   - PC USB port current output varies by computer, which slightly affects charging time. Charging in the **power-off state** is recommended when using a PC USB port to speed up charging.
   - Slight warming of the charger and recorder during charging is normal.

### Power On/Off & Screen Control

- **Power on / off:** Long press the **On/Off (Switch/Play)** key on the right side for **3 seconds**.
- **Screen on / off:** Short press the **On/Off** key after power-on to turn the display off or wake it up.
- **Confirmation key:** In menus, short press the **Switch/Play** key to confirm selections.

---

### Music Playback

From the main interface after boot-up, short press the **Previous** or **Next** key to select the **Music** icon, then short press **Play** to enter the secondary interface:

- **All songs:** Short press the confirmation key to enter the playlist; use Previous/Next to select a track and Play to start playback.
- **Last play:** Short press Play to resume the track currently/last being played.
- **Artists:** Short press Play to browse tracks by artist.
- **Album:** Short press Play to browse tracks by album.
- **My Favorite:** Short press Play to view tracks added to favorites.

#### Playback Controls

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| **Play / Pause** | Short press the **Play** key during playback or pause. |
| **Volume +/-** | During playback, long press the left **Return/VOL** key to enter volume mode, then press **Previous/Next** to adjust volume *(or use dedicated Volume +/- keys on Variant C)*. |
| **Previous / Next track** | Short press **Previous** (`Last Key`) or **Next** (`Next key`). |
| **Fast forward / Fast backward** | Long press **Previous** for fast forward; long press **Next** for fast backward *(as printed in manual)*. |
| **Music submenu** | While playing music, short press the **M Menu** key. |

#### Music Submenu Options

- **Return to home page:** Short press Play to return to the main menu.
- **Variable speed play:** Short press Play; adjust playback speed from **`-9` to `+9`** using Previous/Next, then short press Play to confirm and return to playback.
- **Repeat play:** Short press Play to choose between **Repeat play**, **Random play**, and **Single cycle**. Short press Play to confirm, Return to go back.
- **Bookmark:** Short press Play; supports **5 bookmarks**. Use Previous/Next to select a slot and short press Play to set/play the bookmark.
- **Join my favorite:** Short press Play to toggle adding the current track to My Favorite.
- **Equalizer:** Short press Play to select sound presets (`Normal`, `Pop`, `Rock`, `Jazz`, `Classic`, `Dance`, `Country`, etc.). Short press Play to confirm, then press Back/Return.

#### READ MODE (A-B Repeat) & Icon Indicators

- **Start A-B segment:** While playing music, long press the **M Menu** key — the **"AB"** icon on screen turns **blue** (start point A).
- **Set end point & start loop:** Long press the **Play** key — the **"AB"** icon turns **green** (end point B) and A-B repetition begins.
- **Exit repeat mode:** Long press the **M Menu** key again — the **"AB"** icon turns **white**.

*(On Variant C, long-pressing key 13 also enters A-B repeat mode.)*

### Folder Browser

1. From the main interface, select the **Folder** icon with Previous/Next and short press **Play** to view the folder list and files.
2. When a file or folder is highlighted, press the **M Menu** key to bring up the delete prompt, select **Yes**, and short press **Play** to delete.
3. **Important:** The built-in **`RECORD`** folder cannot be deleted itself; selecting delete on it removes all files *inside* the folder.

---

### Sound Recording

#### One-Click Recording (Hardware Switch)

1. Push the right-side **Recording switch** (`#8`) to the **record** position to start recording immediately.
2. Short press the **Play** key during recording to **pause**; short press **Play** again to **resume**.
3. Push the **Recording switch** back to the **save** position to stop and save the recording.

#### Recording Menu & Settings

From the main interface, select the **Recording** icon and short press **Play**:

1. **Recording Library:** Short press Play to browse recorded files.
2. **Recording Bit Rate:** WAV (PCM linear) with three selectable rates: **`512 kbps`**, **`1536 kbps`**, or **`3072 kbps`**.
3. **Noise Reduction Switch:** Filters out part of the environmental background noise when enabled.
4. **Audio Recording Scene:** Presets for **`close`**, **`interview`**, **`meeting`**, **`station`**, and **`teaching`**.
5. **Recording Segments:** Toggle Off or On with automatic file split intervals of **`30 minutes`**, **`60 minutes`**, or **`120 minutes`**.
6. **Voice-Controlled Recording (VOR / Sound Control):** Toggle Off or On.
   - *Manual note:* When enabled, the recorder automatically starts recording when ambient sound exceeds a threshold and pauses when sound drops below it. Not recommended for important recordings.
7. **Recording Monitoring:** Insert a wired headset first, then toggle On/Off to monitor recording audio in real time through the headset.
8. **Timer Recording:** Configure four parameters: **On/Off**, **Start time**, **End time**, and **Cycle**. Short press Play to save, Return to exit.
   - *Manual note:* System time must be synchronized accurately (printed as "current Beijing time") before using timer recording.

### Bluetooth Modes

From the main interface, select the **Bluetooth** icon and short press **Play** to enter the Bluetooth menu. Short press **Play** to switch between **Music Mode** and **Call Mode**.

#### 1. Music Mode (Bluetooth Audio Source)

- Connects to a **Bluetooth headset** or **Bluetooth speaker** for wireless playback.
- **Paired Devices:** View and reconnect to previously paired devices.
- **Available Devices:** Scan and pair with new Bluetooth audio devices.

#### 2. Call Mode (Phone / WeChat Call Recording)

- Pairs the recorder with a mobile phone to record voice calls (phone or WeChat) directly onto the recorder.
- **Incoming / active calls:** Short press the **Play** key to **answer** an incoming call; short press **Play** again to **hang up**.
- **Native Bluetooth name:** Listed as the last item in the Bluetooth menu (factory default format: **`HW-XX`**).
- **Connection method:** Switch the recorder to Call Mode, enable Bluetooth on the phone, search for the device name displayed on the recorder, and connect.

---

### Settings (`Set up`)

From the main interface, select the **Settings** icon and short press **Play**:

1. **Languages:** Simplified Chinese, English, and other languages.
2. **Time and date:** Set the device date and time.
3. **Brightness:** Adjust display backlight brightness.
4. **Spot screen settings:** Configure screen brightness, screen timeout duration, screensaver mode, and screensaver timeout.
5. **Idle shutdown:** Enable/disable auto power-off and set the idle timeout duration.
6. **Information:** View device / firmware information.
7. **Storage status:** View total and available local storage space.
8. **Factory settings:** Restore factory default settings.
9. **Update Playlist:** Rescan storage and rebuild the music playlist.
10. **Open Secret Code:** Power-on passcode lock toggle (default: **Off**).
11. **Modify password:** Change the device password. Enter the current password first (factory universal default password: **`9999`**), then enter the new password.

## Troubleshooting (`How to deal with common problems`)

| # | Symptom | Recommended Action |
| :-: | --- | --- |
| **1** | **The player cannot be turned on** | Check whether the battery is depleted. Connect to a computer or 5V charger and try turning it on again. |
| **2** | **No sound in the headset** | Verify the volume is not set to zero. Confirm the earphone plug is fully seated in the 3.5 mm jack. |
| **3** | **Severe noise** | Check that the earphone plug is clean (dirt can cause static/noise). Test with other audio files to rule out a corrupted file causing noise or skipping. |
| **4** | **Bluetooth connection unavailable** | Verify the Bluetooth device is paired. Delete all connected devices, toggle Bluetooth off/on or restart the recorder, and restart the target Bluetooth headset/speaker/phone before retrying. |
| **5** | **Garbled characters on screen** | Check that the correct system language is selected in **Settings → Languages**. |
| **6** | **Cannot download / transfer files** | Confirm the USB cable connection between the computer and recorder is secure, and confirm sufficient free storage space *(remainder of item 6 continues past page 21)*. |
